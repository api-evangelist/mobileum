Both gaming and telecom industries face remarkably similar challenges: serving large customer communities, supporting users around the clock, operating across global markets, and delivering consistent experiences without endlessly growing support teams. Over the past decade, gaming companies have shown that AI is far more than an automation tool. It has become part of how they improve customer experiences, streamline operations, and support long-term growth.
