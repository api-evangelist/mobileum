As Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) connectivity moves from demonstration to deployment, connected cars are poised to gain something the industry has pursued for decades: truly ubiquitous connectivity. But delivering on that promise requires more than satellites in orbit. CSPs must ensure that satellite and terrestrial networks work together seamlessly, reliably, and at a global scale.
